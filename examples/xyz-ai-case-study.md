Northstar Digital Health — AI Business Operations Case Study

Fictional case study

Northstar Digital Health is a fictional organization created solely for demonstrating the application of the AI Business Operations Toolkit. It is not based on any current or former employer.

1. Company Context

Northstar Digital Health is a hypothetical global healthcare technology company.

Illustrative profile
5,000 employees
Operations across 12 countries
$2 billion annual revenue
Combination of digital products, technology platforms and professional services
Rapid growth over the previous five years

These figures are entirely illustrative.

2. Business Challenge

Northstar has grown rapidly and now faces increasing operational complexity.

Business leaders have identified several challenges:

Significant time spent preparing management information
Multiple sources of operational data
Manual preparation of reports and presentations
Long decision-making cycles
Increasing demand for productivity improvements
Difficulty identifying opportunities for automation
Growing demand from employees for AI-enabled tools

Leadership wants to determine where AI could create meaningful business value without launching a large-scale technology program prematurely.

3. Initial AI Opportunities

Five potential opportunities have been identified.

Opportunity	Potential Value	Complexity	Initial Assessment
Management reporting assistant	High	Medium	Prioritize
Knowledge search assistant	High	Medium	Prioritize
Automated meeting summaries	Medium	Low	Quick win
Workforce analytics assistant	High	High	Explore
Predictive customer analytics	Very High	High	Investigate

The assessment is illustrative.

4. Opportunity Assessment
Management Reporting Assistant

Problem

Business leaders spend significant time collecting information from multiple sources and preparing recurring management reports.

Potential AI solution

An AI-enabled assistant could help:

Consolidate approved information
Identify trends
Highlight anomalies
Draft management commentary
Prepare questions for leadership review

Potential benefits

Reduced reporting cycle time
Less manual preparation
Faster access to insights
More time available for decision-making

Key risks

Incorrect interpretation of data
Inaccurate generated commentary
Data access and confidentiality
Over-reliance on automated analysis

Human oversight

Final reports and management decisions remain subject to human review.

5. Recommended Pilot

Based on the initial assessment, Northstar should begin with the Management Reporting Assistant.

The pilot should be deliberately small.

Pilot scope

One business unit.

One recurring management report.

One defined data set.

One leadership team.

Success measures

The pilot could measure:

Reporting preparation time
Time from period close to leadership review
Number of manual steps eliminated
Accuracy of generated analysis
User adoption
User satisfaction
6. Implementation Approach
Phase 1 — Discover

Understand the current reporting process and identify pain points.

Phase 2 — Design

Define the target process, AI capability and governance requirements.

Phase 3 — Pilot

Run a controlled pilot with human oversight.

Phase 4 — Measure

Assess business value against predefined success metrics.

Phase 5 — Scale

Expand only if the pilot demonstrates measurable value.

7. Leadership Questions

Before scaling the solution, leadership should ask:

Has measurable business value been demonstrated?
Is the underlying data sufficiently reliable?
Are appropriate security and privacy controls in place?
Is human oversight clearly defined?
Can the solution scale economically?
Will employees adopt the new way of working?
What additional organizational capabilities are required?
Key Lesson

The objective should not be to deploy AI because AI is available.

The objective should be to identify business problems where AI can create measurable value and then scale the solutions that demonstrate that value.

Business problem → AI opportunity → Controlled pilot → Measured value → Responsible scale
