          Executive Decision Principle

          AI investment should be concentrated on opportunities where meaningful business value can be achieved with acceptable risk, practical implementation requirements and a credible path to value. A high number of AI ideas is not itself a reason to invest.

          Scoring Methodology

          Each opportunity is assessed across five dimensions. The scores are normalized to produce a weighted score from 0–100.

          Dimension Weight What it considers
          Business Value 30% Revenue, cost, productivity, experience and risk impact.
          Feasibility 20% Data, technology, skills and organizational readiness.
          Time to Value 20% Expected speed of realizing meaningful benefits.
          Risk 15% Business, regulatory, privacy, security and reputational risk. Higher score means lower risk.
          Strategic Alignment 15% Alignment with important business and strategic priorities.

          Decision Thresholds

          80–100 PRIORITIZE Strong candidate for investment, detailed discovery or pilot planning.
          65–79 EXPERIMENT Promising opportunity suitable for controlled experimentation.
          50–64 INVESTIGATE Further evidence and analysis recommended.
          0–49 DEFER Not currently a priority based on the assessment.

          About This Toolkit

          This is an independently developed professional portfolio project. The toolkit is intended as a decision-support framework rather than a substitute for detailed financial, technology, legal, regulatory or risk analysis.

          The current version focuses on AI opportunity prioritization. Future versions can extend the decision journey into business cases, ROI analysis, investment approval, milestone tracking and portfolio governance.

          All examples are illustrative. No confidential, proprietary, commercially sensitive or employer-specific information is used, collected or stored by this tool.
