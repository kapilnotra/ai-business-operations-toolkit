Scoring Methodology

Each opportunity receives a weighted score from 0–100. The model is designed to support portfolio decision-making, not to replace detailed financial, technical or risk analysis.

Dimension Weight What it considers
Business Value 30% Revenue, cost, productivity, experience and risk impact.
Feasibility 20% Data, technology, skills and organizational readiness.
Time to Value 20% Expected speed of realizing meaningful benefits.
Risk 15% Business, regulatory, privacy, security and reputational risk.
Strategic Alignment 15% Alignment with business strategy and priorities.

Decision Thresholds

80–100 PRIORITIZE Strong candidate for detailed discovery or pilot planning.
65–79 EXPERIMENT Promising opportunity suitable for controlled experimentation.
50–64 INVESTIGATE Further evidence and analysis recommended.
0–49 DEFER Not currently a priority based on the assessment.

About This Toolkit

The AI Investment Decision Toolkit is an independently developed professional portfolio project.

It is intended to help business leaders move from AI ideas toward disciplined investment decisions. It should not be considered a substitute for detailed financial, technology, legal, regulatory or risk analysis.

All examples and scores are illustrative. No confidential, proprietary, commercially sensitive or employer-specific information is collected or stored by this tool.
