    Key Considerations

      Scoring Methodology

      The tool uses a weighted scoring model to provide a consistent framework for comparing potential AI opportunities.

      Dimension Weight What it considers
      Business Value 30% Revenue, cost, productivity, experience and risk impact
      Feasibility 20% Data, technology, skills and organizational readiness
      Time to Value 20% Expected speed of realizing meaningful benefits
      Risk 15% Business, regulatory, privacy, security and reputational risk
      Strategic Alignment 15% Alignment with business strategy and priorities

      Decision Thresholds

      80–100 PRIORITIZE Strong candidate for detailed discovery or pilot planning.
      65–79 EXPERIMENT Promising opportunity suitable for controlled experimentation.
      50–64 INVESTIGATE Further evidence and analysis recommended.
      0–49 DEFER Not currently a priority based on the assessment.

      About This Tool

      This is an independently developed professional portfolio project. The tool is intended as a decision-support framework rather than a substitute for detailed financial, technology, legal, regulatory or risk analysis.

      All examples are illustrative. No confidential, proprietary, commercially sensitive or employer-specific information is used, collected or stored by this tool.
