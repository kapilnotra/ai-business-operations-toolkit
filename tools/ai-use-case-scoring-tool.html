      Scoring Methodology

      The tool uses a weighted scoring model to provide a consistent framework for comparing the attractiveness of potential AI opportunities.

      Dimension Weight What it considers
      Business Value 40% Revenue, cost, productivity, experience, risk and competitive impact.
      Strategic Alignment 15% Alignment with business strategy and strategic priorities.
      Feasibility 15% Data, technology, skills, process maturity and organizational readiness.
      Time to Value 15% Expected speed of realizing meaningful business benefits.
      Risk Manageability 15% Business, regulatory, privacy, security, accuracy and reputational risk.

      Opportunity Attractiveness Calculation

      Opportunity Attractiveness = (Business Value × 40%) + (Strategic Alignment × 15%) + (Feasibility × 15%) + (Time to Value × 15%) + (Risk Manageability × 15%)

      The resulting score is normalized to a 0–100 scale. It measures the attractiveness of the opportunity before detailed financial analysis.

      Decision Thresholds

      80–100 PRIORITIZE Strong candidate for detailed discovery, business-case development or pilot planning.
      65–79 EXPERIMENT Promising opportunity suitable for controlled experimentation and evidence gathering.
      50–64 INVESTIGATE Further evidence and analysis recommended before committing significant resources.
      0–49 DE-PRIORITIZE Not currently a priority relative to competing opportunities.

      Evidence Before Investment

      Probability of success is not treated as a separate weighted scoring dimension. Instead, confidence should be established through evidence.

      Before significant investment, leadership should validate assumptions around technology, data, user adoption, measurable outcomes and implementation feasibility.
