Scoring Methodology

Each opportunity is scored from 1 to 5 across five dimensions. The dimensions are weighted to produce a portfolio score from 0 to 100.

Dimension Weight What it considers
Business Value 30% Potential impact on revenue, cost, productivity, customer experience or business outcomes.
Feasibility 20% Data, technology, skills and organizational readiness.
Time to Value 20% How quickly meaningful benefits could realistically be achieved.
Risk 15% Business, regulatory, privacy, security and reputational risk.
Strategic Alignment 15% Alignment with important business and strategic priorities.
Total 100%

Decision Thresholds

80–100 PRIORITIZE Strong candidate for investment, discovery or pilot planning.
65–79 EXPERIMENT Promising opportunity suitable for controlled experimentation.
50–64 INVESTIGATE Further validation recommended before major investment.
0–49 DE-PRIORITIZE Not currently a priority based on the assessment.

About This Tool

This tool is designed as an independent professional portfolio project. It does not require company-specific information and does not transmit assessment data to a server.

Use illustrative or non-confidential opportunity descriptions. The framework is intended as decision support rather than a substitute for detailed financial, technology, legal, regulatory or risk analysis.
