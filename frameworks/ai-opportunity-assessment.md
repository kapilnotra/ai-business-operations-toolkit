AI Opportunity Assessment Framework
Purpose

A practical framework for business leaders to evaluate where artificial intelligence can create meaningful business value.

The objective is not to identify the largest possible number of AI use cases, but to identify the opportunities where AI can create measurable value while remaining practical, responsible and aligned with business priorities.

1. Define the Business Problem

Start with the business problem rather than the technology.

Key questions:

What problem are we trying to solve?
Who experiences the problem?
How significant is the problem?
How is it currently being managed?
What is the cost of the current approach?
What would success look like?
2. Identify the AI Opportunity

Consider whether AI could improve one or more of the following:

Automate repetitive work
Improve decision-making
Accelerate analysis
Generate content
Improve customer or employee experiences
Predict outcomes
Identify patterns
Support knowledge workers
Reduce operational risk
Improve productivity
3. Assess Business Value

Evaluate the potential value across four dimensions:

Dimension	Key Question
Revenue	Could this increase revenue or growth?
Cost	Could this reduce cost or improve productivity?
Risk	Could this reduce operational or business risk?
Experience	Could this improve customer or employee experience?
4. Assess Feasibility

An attractive idea is not necessarily a practical one.

Assess:

Data availability
Data quality
Technology readiness
Process maturity
Integration requirements
Skills and capabilities
Change requirements
Implementation complexity
5. Assess Risk

Consider:

Data privacy
Security
Accuracy
Bias
Regulatory requirements
Intellectual property
Human oversight
Model reliability
Reputational risk
6. Prioritize the Opportunity

Score each opportunity from 1–5 across:

Business Value

How significant could the benefit be?

Feasibility

How practical is implementation?

Time to Value

How quickly could benefits be realized?

Risk

How significant are the risks?

A simple prioritization approach is:

Priority = Business Value × Feasibility × Time to Value ÷ Risk

This is an illustrative prioritization method rather than a financial valuation methodology.

7. Define the Minimum Viable Use Case

Before investing in a large AI transformation, identify the smallest practical version of the opportunity that can demonstrate value.

Define:

User
Problem
AI capability
Required data
Expected outcome
Success metric
Risk controls
Human oversight
8. Measure Success

Every AI initiative should have measurable outcomes.

Examples include:

Hours saved
Cost reduction
Cycle-time reduction
Revenue generated
Error reduction
Customer satisfaction
Employee experience
Adoption
Quality improvement

The objective is to move from:

"We implemented AI."

to:

"AI created measurable business value."

Summary

A strong AI opportunity should sit at the intersection of:

Business Value




Feasibility




Responsible Implementation




Measurable Outcomes

The framework is designed to help business leaders move from AI experimentation toward disciplined value creation.
